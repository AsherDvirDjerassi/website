Research

I study income and wealth inequality, with a focus on how pensions have shaped the distribution of wealth in the United States.

My work draws on fiscal sociology, stratification, historical institutionalism, and political economy. I am a fellow at the Stone Center for Inequality Dynamics and a core member of the Wealth and Mobility Study.

Background

At Michigan’s Population Studies Center, I received NIH support as a trainee. Before coming to Michigan, I earned an M.S. in data science from the City University of New York and a B.A. in economics from Hampshire College. I also completed a year of graduate coursework in economics at Sciences Po–Paris and spent 2014–2015 in Bulgaria on a Fulbright.
